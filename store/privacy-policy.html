Redlyne — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Summary

Redlyne collects no personal data and makes no network requests. Everything it does happens entirely inside your browser.

Data collected

None. Redlyne does not collect, store, transmit, or share any information about you, your pages, or your edits.

Network requests

Redlyne makes zero network requests. It has no backend, no analytics, no telemetry, and no accounts. The only outbound activity from the extension is the text you deliberately copy to your clipboard and paste into an AI assistant.

Permissions used

  • activeTab / scripting — to inject the properties panel into the current page so you can inspect and edit elements. No page content is ever sent anywhere.
  • storage — to remember your panel preferences (theme, prompt format) in your local browser storage only.
  • clipboardWrite — to copy the generated prompt to your clipboard when you click "Copy changes".

Local storage

Redlyne saves your panel preferences (dark/light mode, prompt format, rules mode, Tailwind hints toggle) to localStorage under the key redlyne-theme and related keys. This data never leaves your device.

Third-party services

None. Redlyne has no dependency on any third-party service.

Open source

Redlyne is open source (AGPL-3.0). You can read every line of code at github.com/ola-fakomi/redlyne.
